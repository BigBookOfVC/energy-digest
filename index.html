Method & scope

  • Window: 1 Jan 2025 to 13 Aug 2026, twenty months.
  • Equity only. Every round here is equity raised by the company. Debt and green-loan facilities, project finance, grants and undrawn capital commitments are excluded entirely, however material the financing event. Where a round blends equity with debt or grant, only the equity portion is counted — and where the split is not disclosed, the card says so.
  • Headquarters in the US, Europe, UK or India only.
  • Each round sits in exactly one of twelve sectors, assigned by what the company sells.
  • Every card links to a named source. Amounts are the equity raised, not cumulative totals.

Sector tests

  • Supply chain — sells to another manufacturer
  • Gen — the output is electricity
  • Storage — sells a deployed storage asset
  • Fuels — the output is a molecule
  • T&D — moving electrons
  • Trading — monetising electrons in a market
  • Efficiency — reduces energy at the load
  • Transport — energy for mobility
  • Industry & heat — decarbonising process heat
  • Ops — running energy assets better
  • Software & fintech — financing and transacting energy
  • Carbon — the output is removed or reused CO2
